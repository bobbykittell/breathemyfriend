Body

Settle the body

A scared body can't hear calm thoughts. Start here.

Stretch

Daily

Your body is wrapped in a web called fascia. Loosen it so stuck tension can let go.

Shake

2× a week

Animals shake after a scare, then move on. Your body knows how to do this too.

Breathe less

Daily

Slow, quiet nose breathing. CO₂ isn't waste. Keeping a little more of it calms you.

Mind

Watch your thoughts

A thought is your brain's guess. Sometimes right, often wrong. You don't have to believe it.

Notice it→ Honor it→ Let it pass

Most feelings pass in about 90 seconds if you don't feed them. If one comes back, that's fine. Watch it again.

Moment

Arrive right here

Stop chasing a better moment later. Thank this one, just as it is.

The buzz of being alive shows up on its own when you stop demanding it. A dull moment counts too.

Go deeper into the moment →

Body first. Then mind. Then the moment.

Why this order? When your body is in alarm, the thinking brain goes partly offline. Settle the body and the mind can finally listen. A quiet mind is what lets you actually be here.
